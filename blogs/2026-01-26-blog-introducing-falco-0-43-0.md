Dear Falco Community, we are happy to announce the release of Falco 0.43.0 today! This is a stabilization release that consolidates the changes introduced in 0.42.0, including the drop-enter initiative and the capture recording feature . It also introduces several deprecations to improve maintainability and fixes minor issues across falcoctl, plugins, and libs.
