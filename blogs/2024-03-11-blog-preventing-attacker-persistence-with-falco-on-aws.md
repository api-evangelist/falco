I recently read an interesting blog on how hackers could use a Lambda function alongside Lex to establish persistence in an AWS account. For those unfamiliar with the term, persistence is when attackers leverage some technique to retain access to systems without being detected. A recent news article cited a study that reported that some Chinese hackers have lurked in systems for up to FIVE YEARS!
