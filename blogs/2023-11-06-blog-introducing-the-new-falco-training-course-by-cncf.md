Detecting Cloud Runtime Threats with Falco (LFS254) is the new Falco training course created by CNCF, Linux Foundation, and Sysdig. We're very excited about this new immersive course designed to enhance your expertise in securing cloud-native applications through hands-on learning. Detecting Cloud Runtime Threats with Falco (LFS254) is a 20-hour course focused on runtime security.
