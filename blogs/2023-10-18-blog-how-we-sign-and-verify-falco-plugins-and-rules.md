Falco v0.36.0 and the Software Supply Chain (SSC) security The latest stable Falco release, v0.36.0, alongside falcoctl 0.6.1 and the 0.7.0 Helm chart introduced new features and improvements to the security of Falco's software supply chain artifacts. Falco's two main downloadable artifacts are plugins and rule sets. They're shipped in the OCI specification format and distributed through the official Falcosecurity OCI repositories.
