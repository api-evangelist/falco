First and foremost, if something goes seriously wrong during Falco deployment, it's usually noticeable immediately. On a longer time scale, continuous performance monitoring and quality assurance, driven by the right metrics, ensure Falco functions as expected 24/7. As a security tool, Falco requires checking for a healthy deployment on multiple dimensions: Resource utilization and system impact: Strive to minimize compute overhead while ensuring the desired monitoring scope is achieved.
