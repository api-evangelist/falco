Dear Falco Community, today we are happy to announce the release of Falco 0.39.0! This version brings several new features, performance improvements, and bug fixes that streamline Falco’s detection capabilities. During this release cycle, we merged more than 50 PRs on Falco and more than 100 PRs for libs and drivers, version 0.
